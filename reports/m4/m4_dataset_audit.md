### Note on SQuAD Metric Handling
SQuAD uses token-level **F1 score**, yielding continuous values in $[0.0, 1.0]$. Coercing SQuAD scores to binary integers ($0$ or $1$) would destroy granular partial credit information. The `eval/m4_data_adapter.py` module preserves continuous floats for SQuAD and verifies strict binary $\{0.0, 1.0\}$ compliance across all other 12 tasks.

## 7. Reconstructed Cost Calculation & Token Statistics

### Cost Formulation
Because historical benchmark runs cannot provide dynamic provider invoices, costs are reconstructed from observed token usage and official candidate pricing rates:

$$\text{cost}_{\text{USD}} = \frac{\text{input\_tokens} \times \text{input\_price\_per\_1m} + \text{output\_tokens} \times \text{output\_price\_per\_1m}}{1{,}000{,}000}$$

* Exact precision is computed using `Decimal` fixed-point arithmetic (`eval/m4_data_adapter.py`).
* **Disclaimer:** Reconstructed costs represent benchmark-derived theoretical expenditures based on published token prices. They do not represent live billing receipts.

## 8. Evaluation Aggregation Methodology

### Task Macro-Average (Primary Metric)
In xRouteBench, task prompt volumes are unbalanced in the test set: tasks like `math`, `gsm8k`, and `mmlu` each have 500 test queries (9,000 observations), whereas `aime_2020_2024` has 13 queries (234 observations) and `human_eval` has 16 queries (288 observations).

A simple micro-average would allow large benchmarks to dominate router rankings. To ensure fair multi-domain evaluation:

$$\text{Macro-Score} = \frac{1}{13} \sum_{t=1}^{13} \left( \frac{1}{|D_t|} \sum_{i \in D_t} y_i \right)$$

### Micro-Average (Secondary Metric)
Micro-average is computed as the unweighted mean over all observations:

$$\text{Micro-Score} = \frac{1}{|D|} \sum_{i \in D} y_i$$

### Cluster-Bootstrap Confidence Intervals
For reporting statistical significance:
* Bootstrap resampling is performed at the **prompt/query level** (cluster bootstrap) rather than the row level.
* Resampling queries with replacement preserves the joint multi-model evaluations for each selected prompt.
